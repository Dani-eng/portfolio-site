Computational and dynamics work spanning fluid mechanics, aerospace-related systems, and engineering applications.

CFD · 2023

Vortex patterns around an oscillating cylinder

As the sole contributor, I conceived and developed the investigation, ran OpenFOAM simulations in parallel on the Unity cluster, and carried out post-processing. I searched for symmetric vortex shedding comparable to patterns observed in high-fidelity, high-Reynolds-number simulations.

OpenFOAM Unity Cluster HPC Bash scripting Vortex-induced motion Post-processing

Nonlinear dynamics · 2022

Harvesting energy from pendulum motion

As the sole contributor, I conceived and developed the study and performed the analysis and post-processing in MATLAB. I examined single- and double-pendulum behavior across periodic and chaotic regimes to investigate how chaotic pendulum systems can produce energy for harvesting.

MATLAB Nonlinear dynamics Chaotic systems Energy harvesting Post-processing

Fluid–structure interaction · 2020

Paraglider flight as a coupled system

As the sole contributor, I conceived and developed a MATLAB model of paragliding as a fluid–structure interaction system and performed the post-processing. I examined how pilot position, line stiffness, and weight relate to stability and the onset of instability.

MATLAB Fluid–structure interaction Aerodynamics Structural response Stability analysis
✳
Published research · 2025

Computational assessment of coronary flow

As a joint contributor, I created coronary-artery CAD geometry from medical images, prepared the geometry and mesh, ran CFD simulations, and performed post-processing and analysis. The study used proper orthogonal decomposition; higher modes may help medical professionals assess coronary flow when placing stents.

Medical-image processing ANSYS SpaceClaim Meshing OpenFOAM Proper orthogonal decomposition CFD analysis
❄
Published research · 2020

Solidification modeling with an extended-FEM approach

I extended an existing finite-element code by adding an energy model and XFEM capabilities to enable solidification modeling. This involved Fortran development in an in-house CFD code and supported prediction of the solidification front in multiphase fluid–structure interaction cases, with relevance to ice buildup on aircraft components.

Fortran In-house CFD code XFEM Energy model Solidification Multiphase FSI
